# BagSmith AI Submission Pack

## Project Name

BagSmith AI

## Short Description

An AI launch operating system that helps creators design, launch, and grow Bags-native creator coins.

"Smith" means crafting. BagSmith AI helps creators craft better Bags coin launches.

## Long Description

BagSmith AI helps creators turn an idea for a Bags creator coin into a clear launch plan. Creators enter a brief about their audience, goal, tone, and desired coin utilities. The app generates a responsible launch kit with positioning, coin utility rules, Bags integration guidance, campaign posts, community tasks, drops, and risk notes.

The MVP also includes real Phantom wallet connection and a live Solana token mint lookup, giving the demo a real Web3 surface instead of staying as a static AI writing tool.

For the hackathon requirement, BagSmith AI should be launched on Bags. Add the live Bags project URL here before final submission:

```text
Bags project URL: https://bags.fm/$ZHOUFUI
```

## Why It Matters

Creator coins can help creators coordinate fans, contributors, and communities, but many launches are confusing or too speculative. BagSmith AI makes launches more structured, more useful, and easier to understand. It helps Bags attract better creator coin projects by giving creators an operating system for design, launch, and growth.
